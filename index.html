About

Building reliable AI with structure, knowledge, and uncertainty.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Biomedical Data Science at Dartmouth College, where I work on AI methods for whole-body PET/CT analysis, cancer lesion segmentation, self-supervised learning, domain generalization, and uncertainty quantification. My broader research agenda is to move beyond purely data-driven prediction by incorporating prior knowledge, physical and anatomical constraints, and calibrated uncertainty into learning systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical, Computer, and Systems Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), where my research focused on probabilistic scene graph generation, structured prediction, and uncertainty-aware visual reasoning. My work spans medical imaging, computer vision, probabilistic machine learning, and multimodal AI.

Research

Research vision

My long-term goal is to develop AI systems that are not only accurate, but also aware of what they know, constrained by domain structure, and capable of integrating multiple forms of evidence.

01

Prior Knowledge

Integrating anatomical, spatial, relational, and population-level priors into deep learning systems to improve sample efficiency and generalization.

02

Physics & Domain Constraints

Embedding meaningful constraints from imaging physics and domain structure so learned models produce more plausible and robust predictions.

03

Model Uncertainty

Quantifying aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty and using uncertainty as an actionable signal for model fusion, data selection, and clinical decision support.

Medical AI · PET/CT

Uncertainty-aware whole-body lesion segmentation

Developing uncertainty-augmented segmentation approaches for multi-tracer PET/CT, including FDG and PSMA imaging, to improve robustness across lesion burden, cancer types, and radiotracers.

Foundation Models · SSL

Label-efficient learning across tracers

Exploring self-supervised and foundation-model-based strategies for learning transferable representations from large unlabeled PET/CT cohorts and reducing annotation requirements.

Prior-Informed AI

Anatomical and population-level priors

Combining deep neural predictions with anatomical atlases and population-level metastatic distributions to improve clinically meaningful lesion detection.

Computer Vision · Structured AI

Probabilistic scene graph generation

Modeling relationships between visual entities using Bayesian networks, structured probabilistic models, temporal reasoning, and uncertainty-aware inference.
